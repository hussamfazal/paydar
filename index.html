Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email me.

Does Paydar collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. What you enter is stored on your iPhone, and copied to your Apple Watch if you use the watch app. Links you open (a provider's cancel page, your Apple subscriptions) open in Safari or the App Store. Full details are in the privacy policy.

My reminders aren't arriving

Go through these in order:

  1. Allow notifications. On your iPhone: Settings, Notifications, Paydar, and turn on Allow Notifications.
  2. Turn reminders on in the app. Open Paydar, tap the gear, and switch on "Remind me before charges". Check the "When" and "At" settings there.
  3. Check the subscription. Open it and look at "Remind me". It can be off, on the day, 1 day, 3 days or 1 week before.
  4. Try the test. In the gear screen, tap "Send a test reminder". It arrives in about 5 seconds. It reads like your next real reminder; until you have set a charge date it is a plain "Reminders are working". If it arrives, reminders work and the real ones are waiting for their day.
  5. Check Focus and Notification Summary. A Focus mode or a scheduled summary can hide or delay a reminder.

Good to know: a charge date that is only a guess (quick add guesses one) gets no reminder until you set it, so tap "Set the dates" on Home. If you add something after its usual reminder time has already passed, Paydar sends one reminder about a minute later. iOS lets an app keep 64 reminders scheduled at once, so Paydar schedules the nearest ones, tops them up each time you open the app and in the background when iOS allows, and the last one asks you to open Paydar. If you track many subscriptions and haven't opened Paydar for a few months, open it once.

The Apple Watch app isn't showing my subscriptions
  1. Make sure the Paydar watch app is installed: on your iPhone open the Watch app, find Paydar, and turn on "Show App on Apple Watch".
  2. Open Paydar on your iPhone, then open Paydar on your watch. Keep the iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on.
  3. The watch gets a fresh copy whenever something changes on the iPhone. If it still looks old, change anything small on the iPhone (or just reopen the app) and look again.
  4. If it is still empty, restart both devices and open the iPhone app first.

The watch app only shows your list. You add and edit subscriptions on the iPhone. When your iPhone is out of reach, the watch shows the last list it received.

How do I import a spreadsheet (CSV)?

Open Paydar, tap the gear, then "Import from a spreadsheet", and choose your file. Paydar shows what it found; tap "Add" to bring them in.

The first row must be a header. Required columns: name, amount, cycle (weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly) and next_charge (a date like 2026-10-21). Optional columns: plan, category, status, service, notes. Amounts can be written 9.99, $9.99 or 9,99.

If one row has a problem, Paydar adds nothing and tells you which row. The easiest start: add one subscription by hand, tap "Export to a spreadsheet", and use that file as your template.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Paydar can't cancel for you. It takes you to the right page.

  1. Open the subscription in Paydar and tap "How to cancel". Depending on the service it opens the provider's own page, your Apple subscriptions, or a web search.
  2. If the service is billed through Apple: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose the one you want and tap Cancel.
  3. Come back to Paydar. It asks "Did you cancel?". Tap "Yes, I canceled" and it stops counting it and shows what you save each year.

Deleting a subscription in Paydar only removes it from the app. It never cancels anything with the provider.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every App Store purchase, so refunds come from Apple, not from me. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with, and choose Paydar. Apple decides refund requests under its own policy. If something is broken, please email me first: I would rather fix it.

Do the typical prices fill in for my country?

When your currency is US or Canadian dollars, Paydar fills in a typical price for most of the popular services (checked on 7 October 2026). A few popular services have no price we could confirm in Canada, and most of the less common ones don't fill in a price, so you type those. They are list prices for the usual plan and may be out of date, so change any price to match your bill. In any other currency you type the price yourself.

Does it connect to my bank or email to find subscriptions?

No. Paydar never asks for a bank login or email access, which is why nothing about you leaves your phone. The trade-off is that you add what you pay for yourself. The 102 built-in services make that quick: tap the logos you have.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Before you switch, open Paydar, tap the gear, and tap "Export to a spreadsheet" to save a copy. On the new iPhone, install Paydar and use "Import from a spreadsheet". If you restore the new iPhone from a backup, Paydar's data may come across with it, as it does for most apps.

What does it need to run?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The Apple Watch app is optional and needs watchOS 26 or later. There is no iPad version. The app is in English.
